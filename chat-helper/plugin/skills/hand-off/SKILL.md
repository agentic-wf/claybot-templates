# Hand off

Hand off, rather than attempt, when the request:

- needs a decision a person owns (priorities, hiring, spending, policy);
- would act outside your workspace with consequences — deploying, deleting
  shared data, sending external email, changing permissions;
- needs access or credentials you do not have;
- is something you tried and could not resolve.

How:

1. Say plainly that you are not the right one to do it, and why, in one line.
2. Give what you can: the options, a draft, the relevant facts you found.
3. Name who or what can help if you know it (from `NOTES.md`, a CODEOWNERS
   file, or the thread), without guessing at people.
4. Never pretend to have done something you did not do.

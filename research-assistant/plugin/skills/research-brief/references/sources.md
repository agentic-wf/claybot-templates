# Weighing sources

Strongest first:

1. The primary source: official documentation, the standard or law itself,
   the paper, the repository and its changelog, the company's filing.
2. Direct measurement you ran (a command, a benchmark), with the command.
3. Reputable secondary reporting that cites a primary source — follow the
   citation and cite that instead when you can.
4. Blog posts, forums, and answers: leads only; confirm before citing.

Always check the date. For software, prefer the docs of the current version;
say which version a claim applies to. When two good sources disagree, report
both and what explains the difference.

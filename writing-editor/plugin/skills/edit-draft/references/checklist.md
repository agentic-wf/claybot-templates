# Editing checklist

**Structure**
- The first sentence says the point or the ask.
- Each paragraph does one job; headings or bullets where a reader scans.
- Anything the reader must do is explicit: what, by when.

**Sentences**
- Under about 25 words; split the ones that are not.
- Active voice unless the actor does not matter.
- No stacked qualifiers ("we believe it may potentially").

**Words**
- Cut: very, really, just, basically, in order to, it is important to note.
- One term per thing; do not alternate synonyms for variety.
- Jargon only when the reader uses it too.

**Tone**
- Match the channel: a chat message is not a press release.
- Confident where the author is sure; honest where they are not.
